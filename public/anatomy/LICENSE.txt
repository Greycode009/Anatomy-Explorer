BodyParts3D 3.0 (20110915).

Citation: Mitsuhashi N, Fujieda K, Tamura T, Kawamoto S, Takagi T, Okubo K.
BodyParts3D: 3D structure database for anatomical concepts. Nucleic Acids Res. 2009;37:D782-5.
doi:10.1093/nar/gkn613

Modifications: converted from STL to GLB, vertices welded, decimated with meshoptimizer
(realistic and educational levels of detail), re-oriented and scaled to metres, grouped into
structures, quantized and meshopt-compressed (scripts/prepare-assets.mjs).
